What it means

The world is moving to small families, but not in step: in 2023 half the countries were below replacement while some still averaged six children. So we ask when each country changes, and what goes with that moment.

UN World Population Prospects 2024 · 1950–2023 estimates; dashed = UN projections · every excluded place is listed by name (excluded_places.csv)

In 2023, the total fertility rate was 0.72 in the Republic of Korea and 6.06 in Niger. The histograms show the shift: in 1950, only 3 of 200 countries were below replacement; by 2023, 103. So we asked: do countries follow the same path, and what moves with their turning point: income, education, or health? Our data is UN World Population Prospects 2024. In the funnel, summing every row gives 363 billion people, because regions repeat countries; countries alone give 8.09 billion. We keep 200 countries, 99.98 percent of the world, list every place we leave out, and draw UN projections dashed.
