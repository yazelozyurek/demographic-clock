Reproducibility

Python 3.13 (pandas, NumPy, SciPy, statsmodels, Matplotlib). Notebooks 01–06 and the scripts in pilot/ and kesif/ reproduce every number and figure; each test's plan was committed before its results. excluded_places.csv names every place left out, with the reason. Code: private GitHub repository, made public after the competition.

Use of AI

We used an AI assistant as a working tool: to write analysis code, propose and test methods, design the figures and draft the slides and narration. The team chose the questions, made the final decisions, reviewed and edited the AI-written code, and re-ran all of it before submission to confirm that every number reproduces.
