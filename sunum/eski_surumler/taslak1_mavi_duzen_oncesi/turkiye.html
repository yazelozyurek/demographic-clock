What we do not claim

· Causes: these are patterns

· Individual-level claims

· That UN figures are observed

· Results for pre-1950 turns

· That further along is better

· That projections are forecasts

What it means

Türkiye’s decline began while young women’s schooling was still low (3.6 vs a typical 5.5 years). Its population likely peaks near 2047; for 2100, plan with the range (26–125 million), not one number.

Guided by the ASA Ethical Guidelines for Statistical Practice (2022) · UN WPP 2024, Medium and probabilistic variants

Türkiye turned around 1982. It was richer than 45 of these 66 countries at their turning points, but its young women had less schooling than 54 of them. The UN projects Türkiye’s population to peak in 2047 at 91 million. By 2100 its 95 percent interval runs from 26 to 125 million, so we show a range, not a number. Our limits: these are country-level patterns, not causes, and they say nothing about individual women. Many UN figures are themselves model estimates where registration is weak. Countries that turned before 1950 are outside the money-or-school test. And the clock is not a race: being further along is not better.
